Rei Li

Electrical Engineering
New York University · Tandon School of Engineering · Brooklyn, NY

I am an Electrical Engineering student at NYU Tandon School of Engineering. My research centers on energy-efficient and secure computing hardware, with an emphasis on FPGA acceleration, reconfigurable architectures, and embedded systems for edge intelligence. I also work on real-time control and energy cyber-physical systems, which provides a broader system-level perspective on dependable hardware design.

My long-term goal is to develop efficient, secure, and dependable hardware systems that bridge intelligent algorithms with practical digital implementation.
