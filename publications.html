Predictive Impedance-Shaped Synchronization for Island-to-Grid Reconnection of Grid-Forming DER Inverters

IEEE IECON 2026 Accepted
Proposes a PLL-free predictive impedance-shaped controller for smooth island-to-grid reconnection of grid-forming DER inverters. By minimizing predicted closing current before reconnection, it achieves 3.7 times faster synchronization than PLL control while reducing peak current from 14.56 A to 2.36 A in an independent-closure benchmark.
