Current Project

● Ongoing  ·  Undergraduate Thesis

A Chaotic ROI-Selective Image Encryption Accelerator for the Xilinx Kria KV260, Co-Designed with YOLOv8-nano Detection

Developing a chaotic ROI-selective image encryption accelerator for the Xilinx Kria KV260, co-designed with YOLOv8-nano object detection. Detected regions of interest guide selective encryption, linking visual detection with hardware-based image protection.

The project focuses on integrating detection outputs with the FPGA encryption pipeline and exploring the trade-offs between resource use, processing latency, and selective protection for edge vision applications.

Embedded AI Systems

Independent Study · NYU

ESP32-Based Intelligent Assistive Platform

Designing a resource-constrained embedded AI platform centered on the ESP32 microcontroller, targeting human-centered assistive applications. The system integrates a camera module for real-time computer vision inference, a microphone array with voice activity detection, and multiple environmental sensors fused through a lightweight Kalman-based estimator.

Key design constraints include sub-100 mW power budget, latency under 200 ms for vision inference, and operation without cloud connectivity. Inference is handled on-device using quantized models optimized for the ESP32's dual-core Xtensa architecture.

Real-Time Control & Energy Systems

NYU Power & Energy Systems Group · Prof. Francisco de León

Adaptive Control for Inverter-Based Microgrids

Developed a suite of adaptive control and synchronization algorithms for inverter-based distributed energy resources (DER) in grid-connected and islanded microgrid configurations. Work spans three related problems: frequency regulation under variable photovoltaic generation, seamless island-to-grid reconnection, and battery-aware black-start restoration.

The SoC-aware phase-angle control framework extends classical droop control by incorporating a real-time SoC feedback loop that adjusts the inverter's active power setpoint, preventing deep discharge events while maintaining grid frequency within IEEE 1547 bounds. Simulations conducted in PSCAD and MATLAB/Simulink.

Battery Management Systems for Electric Vehicles

Investigated lithium-ion battery modeling, state estimation, and thermal management strategies for electric vehicle battery packs. Work included equivalent circuit model identification, SoC estimation using extended Kalman filtering, and analysis of capacity fade under cycling conditions.

Research Vision

My long-term goal is to develop efficient intelligent hardware systems that integrate machine learning algorithms directly with digital hardware — moving AI from cloud servers to the silicon edge. I am particularly interested in FPGA-based AI acceleration, hardware security architectures, and energy-efficient systems capable of supporting real-time applications under strict power and latency constraints.


I am currently expanding expertise in RTL design, FPGA architecture, timing analysis, computer architecture, and ASIC design flows — aiming to contribute to next-generation computing platforms at the hardware–algorithm interface.
